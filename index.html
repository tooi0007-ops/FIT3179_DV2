02 · The recovery

Forty years of growth stopped almost overnight

Australian passenger numbers grew for almost forty years.
Two major disruptions changed that long-term pattern.

Total passenger movements are shown from 1985 to 2025.
Hover the highlighted years for exact values.

Recovery to the previous peak:
2025 was only 0.2% above 2019.
Six years of population growth had passed.

The 1989 pilots' dispute disrupted domestic aviation.
This event explains the sharp decline.
