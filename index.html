Australian Airport Activity

Exploring how passenger movements, airport size, aircraft activity and domestic routes vary across Australia from 2019 to 2025.

Airport map National trend Airport change Domestic routes Sources

01

Passenger activity is concentrated in a small number of cities

Compare airport scale, state-level change and the busiest domestic connections across Australia.

Where is airport activity concentrated?

Circle area shows total passenger movements in 2025. One circle represents one airport; this map focuses on mainland Australia and Tasmania.

How did passenger movements change across states?

Colour shows percentage change in passenger movements at airports with verified terminal locations, from 2019 to 2025. 0% means the same passenger-movement level as 2019.

Which domestic routes carried the most passengers?

Curved-line width shows combined passenger movements on the ten busiest domestic city-pair routes in 2025; dots and labels mark airport endpoints.

Melbourne–Sydney was the largest corridor, with 8.1 million passenger movements. Lines have no direction because each source route combines both travel directions.

02

Australian air travel grew over the long term, with a sharp disruption in 2020

Track national passenger movements from 1985 to 2025 and place recent airport activity in its longer-term context.

Showing total passenger movements. The annotations identify the 2020 disruption and the return to the 2019 level.

03

Airport activity differs by passenger volume, travel type and aircraft use

Compare changes in airport size, domestic and international activity, ranking, and passenger movements per aircraft movement.

Which large airports changed most between 2019 and 2025?

Each line compares total passenger movements in 2019 and 2025 for one of the 15 largest airports.

Largest contrast: Sunshine Coast grew 34.7% from 2019 to 2025, while Canberra fell 13.7%.

How does domestic and international activity differ by airport?

The left side is domestic and the right side is international passenger movements in 2025.

Domestic dependence: Hobart recorded 2.87 million domestic passenger movements but only 19,580 international movements in 2025 (99.3% domestic).

How did airport passenger ranks change over time?

Rank 1 is largest. The same twelve airports are followed from 2019 to 2025.

Key change: Sunshine Coast fell to rank 16 in 2020, then returned to rank 11 by 2025.

Which airports had the most passengers per aircraft movement?

This ratio compares passenger movements with recorded aircraft movements at the 20 busiest airports in 2025. It is not an airline load factor.

How is passenger activity distributed across states and airports?

Australia → state or territory → airport. Tile area shows 2025 passenger movements at airports with verified terminal locations.

04

Major domestic routes show different monthly and long-term patterns

Compare recent monthly passenger movements on major routes with their average levels before 2020.

How have major domestic routes changed by month?

Colour compares the average monthly passenger movements in 2023–25 with the same month in 2015–19.
Only complete and published values are included.

Strongest growth: Gold Coast–Melbourne was 17.3% above its 2015–19 average in February.

Data notes

Passenger figures are airport passenger movements, not unique travellers.
State totals include airports with verified terminal-location matches.
Route values unavailable for release are retained as missing.
