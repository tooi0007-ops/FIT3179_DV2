01 · The network

A few major cities carry most of Australia's air travel

Airport scale is concentrated around the capital cities, while the strongest domestic connections form an east-coast spine.

Where is airport activity concentrated?

Circle area shows total passenger movements in 2025. One circle represents one airport; this map focuses on mainland Australia and Tasmania.

Scale is concentrated: Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane together recorded 103.8 million passenger movements in 2025.

02 · The recovery

Forty years of growth were interrupted almost overnight

The national total recovered by 2025, but domestic and international travel followed different paths back.

Showing total passenger movements. The annotations identify the 2020 disruption and the return to the 2019 level.

Back to the headline level: 2025 exceeded 2019 by only 0.2%, despite six years of population and economic growth.

The sharp 1989 fall coincided with the Australian pilots' dispute, which heavily disrupted domestic aviation. BITRE context.

03 · The airports

National recovery did not reach every airport equally

Some leisure-focused airports grew beyond 2019, while several capital-city airports remained smaller or changed their travel mix.

Which states moved beyond their 2019 passenger levels?

Red means passenger movements remained below 2019; blue means they moved above it. Totals include airports with verified terminal locations.

Recovery was uneven: Western Australia grew 19.5%, while the ACT remained 13.7% below 2019.

Which large airports changed most between 2019 and 2025?

Each line runs from the 2019 baseline (0%) to the percentage change in total passenger movements in 2025 for one of the 15 largest airports.

Largest contrast: Sunshine Coast grew 34.7% from 2019 to 2025, while Canberra fell 13.7%.

Sunshine Coast's new 2,450-metre runway opened in June 2020, allowing larger aircraft and more direct routes—important context for its later growth. Airport expansion source.

How did the largest airports move through the rankings?

Unlike the percentage-change chart above, this bump chart follows each airport's relative position from 2015 to 2025. Direct labels identify every line; Sunshine Coast is highlighted.

A gradual climb: Sunshine Coast moved from 14th in 2015 to 11th in 2025, while the five largest airports kept the same order.

How does domestic and international activity differ by airport?

The left side is domestic and the right side is international passenger movements in 2025.

Domestic dependence: Hobart recorded 2.87 million domestic passenger movements but only 19,580 international movements in 2025 (99.3% domestic).

04 · The routes

Route recovery depends on where—and when—people fly

Recent monthly averages reveal a split: some leisure corridors grew, while several major business routes still trail their pre-2020 levels.
